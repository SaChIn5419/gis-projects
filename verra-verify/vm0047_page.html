VM0047 Afforestation, Reforestation, and Revegetation, v1.1

Details
Status Active Since 14 May 2025
Sectoral Scope
14. Forestry and other land use (forests, wetlands, and grasslands)
Outcome Labels Removals

This methodology applies to afforestation, reforestation, and revegetation (ARR) activities that establish, increase, or restore vegetative cover in non-forest areas or enhance forest carbon stocks in areas with existing forest cover. The methodology quantifies carbon removals from these activities and provides two approaches for quantifying such carbon removals:

This approach applies to ARR projects that change land cover from non-forest to forest or enhance stocks in existing forests. Detectable changes in vegetative cover measured using remote sensing and plot-based sampling serve as the basis for quantifying carbon removals. This approach uses a dynamic performance benchmark (PB) to assess additionality and determine the crediting baseline at each verification. The PB is derived from ex-post observations that compare the change in the vegetative stocking index (SI) between remotely sensed project and matched control plots.

The census-based approach applies to ARR projects that do not occur in forests and do not result in land use changes. This approach enables the inclusion of ARR activities that would otherwise be difficult to cover under the area-based approach and where a full census of plantings is feasible. This approach best suits dispersed planting activities (e.g., urban forestry, agroforestry, shelterbelts, and revegetation activities that do not meet the forest definition). Under this approach, additionality is demonstrated using a project method and to determine the crediting baseline. Under the census-based approach, projects are exempt from applying leakage deduction because projects do not result in a change to existing land use.

VMD0054 Module for Estimating Leakage from ARR Activities must be applied in conjunction with projects that use the area-based approach to account for leakage related to the displacement of pre-project agricultural activities caused by the baseline agent or other actors.

3.1 Spatial Resolution

The SI dataset provided for projects must have final spatial resolution finer than 30 m × 30 m (i.e., pixel size ≤ 30 m) for all years provided. Describe your source data and processing steps that ensure the delivered SI maintains this resolution.

3.2 Temporal Resolution

The SI must be available at least annually, covering relevant baseline and monitoring years for the project. Describe the cadence at which the SI is produced and any constraints or gaps.

3.3 Seasonality Control

Optical imagery used to generate the SI should be constrained to a consistent seasonal window each year to limit phenology and atmospheric effects. Describe the seasonal window(s) and quality filters you apply.

Grace Period for Transition to VM0047 v1.1
Verra recognizes that some projects have already been designed under the assumptions of VM0047 v1.0. To support a smooth transition, the following effective dates and grace periods apply:

  • As of May 14, 2025, VM0047 v1.1 is effective and may be used for all projects.
  • On May 14, 2025, Verra also released Corrections and clarifications to VM0047, v1.0 (PDF) which are effective immediately.  Changes are also provided in a greenlined format for convenience: VM0047 Afforestation, Reforestation, and Revegetation, v1.0 – Greenlined (PDF),
  • The following grace periods apply to all projects using VM0047, v1.0:
    • Projects must request pipeline listing as Under Validation on the Verra Registry by December 31, 2025.
    • Projects must request registration on the Verra Registry by December 31, 2026.

Grace period for projects using AR-ACM0003 or AR-AMS0007
Projects using AR-ACM0003 or AR-AMS0007 listed as Under Validation must complete validation no later than June 30, 2025, as per the announcement from November 1, 2024.

ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (CCP) Label Eligibility

VM0047, v1.1 and VM0047, v1.0 have been approved by the Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market (ICVCM) as meeting the requirements of the Core Carbon Principles (CCPs) Assessment Framework. See the ICVCM decision for VM0047, v1.1 (external) and VM0047, v1.0 (external). A guidance document outlining Verra’s process for CCP labeling, the ICVCM CCP Label Guidance (PDF), is available on the  VCS Rules and Requirements page.

Where VCUs have been issued from projects using multiple methodologies, only VCUs from projects solely using CCP-approved methodologies are eligible for CCP labels.

VM0047 Methodology for Afforestation, Reforestation, and Revegetation, v1.1
Status: Active since May 14, 2025
Mitigation Outcome Label Eligibility: Removals
Summary of Changes:

  • Project activities are now allowed on forested lands, provided the area has not been managed for wood products.
  • The methodology clarifies that the census-based approach applies to projects not expected to cause land-use change, with the threshold for land-use change set at more than 50 planting units per hectare and no restrictions on the size of project instances.
  • Projects may now use remote sensing to estimate pre-existing woody biomass at the start date, under specific conditions.
  • Projects using the census-based approach may use GPS coordinates or physical markers to identify trees.
  • Projects may now remove pre-existing woody biomass offsite in area-based approaches, provided it is measured (via physical or remote sensing methods) and fully accounted for as part of project emissions.
  • The methodology has been updated throughout to improve clarity, consistency, and ease of interpretation.

Developer(s): Verra. Per section 2.1.2 of the MDRP, Verra applied an alternative process and authored this revision.
